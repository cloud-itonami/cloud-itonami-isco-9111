# Business Model: Independent Domestic Cleaning Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-9111`
- ISCO-08: `9111`
- Occupation: Domestic Cleaners and Helpers
- Social impact: local-jobs, household-support, worker-safety

## Customer

- households
- short-term rental hosts
- small offices

## Offer

- scheduled cleaning visits
- supply restocking
- service checklists
- access logging
- quality inspection

## Revenue

- per-visit fee
- monthly service-plan fee
- reporting package

## Trust Controls

- no cleaning-robot operation in occupied rooms without consent
- chemical-agent handling requires human sign-off
- access logs are auditable, not editable
